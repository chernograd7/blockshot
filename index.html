BLOCK SHOT

A pixel online shooter for mobile: team battles and duels on blocky maps.

  • 5 vs 5 team deathmatch and round-based 1 vs 1 duels
  • Buy menu with weapons and armour, grenades, shotguns and sniper rifles
  • In-game chat with emoji
  • Google account: your nickname, game ID and inventory are saved
